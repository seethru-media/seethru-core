# Welcome to seethru.media

We're building something different.

## The Problem

Modern news platforms optimize for engagement, not understanding. They use hidden algorithms to maximize time-on-site, often at the cost of truth and mental health.

## Our Solution

**seethru.media** is built on radical transparency:

- **No cookies or tracking** — Your reading habits are private
- **Open-source algorithms** — See exactly how content is ranked
- **Geographic daily briefs** — News organized by location, not outrage
- **Visible attribution** — Every piece shows who made it and who funded it
- **Community moderation** — Public jury decisions, not invisible admins

## What's Different?

### 1. You Own Your Attention

We don't use engagement optimization. No infinite scroll designed for addiction. Just a daily brief of what matters in your area.

### 2. Transparency First

- Our ranking code is public
- Our finances are public
- Our moderation decisions are public
- Our governance documents are forkable

### 3. Liberal Moderation

We only have 4 absolute red lines:
- CSAM
- Direct incitement to violence
- Doxxing
- Fraud/impersonation

Everything else goes to a community jury. No auto-censoring "toxicity."
