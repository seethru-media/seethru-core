# Trust Principles: Why Transparency Matters

Our platform is built on six core commitments to readers. Here's what they mean in practice.

## 1. You Own Your Attention

**The problem:** Most platforms optimize for engagement, not understanding. They use psychological tricks to keep you scrolling, often showing you content that makes you angry because anger drives clicks.

**Our commitment:**
- No engagement optimization algorithms
- No infinite feeds designed for addiction
- Geographic daily brief first, rabbit holes second

## 2. You Know Who Made This

**The problem:** Anonymous content can spread unchecked. You don't know if the author has conflicts of interest or if AI wrote it.

**Our commitment:**
- Every piece shows author, source, and date
- AI-generated content is always labeled
- No anonymous accusations of named individuals

## 3. We Don't Read Your Messages

**The problem:** Many platforms scan your private messages for moderation or advertising.

**Our commitment:**
- End-to-end encrypted DMs
- We cannot access message content
- We only act on user reports

## 4. We Don't Track Without Disclosure

**The problem:** Most websites track everything you do, building profiles to sell to advertisers.

**Our commitment:**
- No cookies
- All tracked data visible to you
- Full opt-out available
- We never sell data

## 5. Our Finances Are Public

**The problem:** You don't know who funds most news platforms or where their money goes.

**Our commitment:**
- See our costs and revenue in real-time
- Know who funds us
- Understand where money goes

## 6. Governance is Forkable

**The problem:** Platform rules change arbitrarily with no input from users.

**Our commitment:**
- All rules are public and versioned
- Anyone can propose changes
- If you don't like our governance, fork it

---

These aren't just nice words. They're enforced by our Platform Charter and public accountability.

If we violate these principles, you can hold us accountable or fork the project and do it better.
